# Code Of Conduct

L5E discussions should stay technical, specific and useful.

Expected behavior:

- focus criticism on code, behavior and tradeoffs
- assume maintainers may decline features that do not fit the framework scope
- provide reproducible examples for bugs
- keep disagreement concise and respectful

Unacceptable behavior:

- insults, harassment or personal attacks
- repeated demands after a maintainer has closed a decision
- derailing issues into unrelated framework debates
- publishing private information

Maintainers may lock threads, close issues or block accounts to keep the project usable.
